## 🎯 Tips for Staying Consistent

1. **Pomodoro for focus**: 50 min work, 10 min break.
2. **Track your progress**: Use Notion/Excel to log problems solved.
3. **Revise frequently**: Re-do old problems to retain patterns.
4. **Mock Interviews**: Try [Pramp](https://www.pramp.com/), [Interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/).
5. **Join community**: Discords, Reddit, Telegram for accountability.
